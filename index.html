Slide Translator

Slide Translator is a Google Slides add-on that creates a translated copy of your presentation in the language of your choice — leaving your original deck untouched.

Features

  • Translates slide text, tables, and speaker notes
  • Creates a new copy, so your original presentation is never modified
  • Optional bilingual mode — keep the original text alongside the translation
