Privacy Policy for Slide Translator

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Slide Translator is a Google Slides add-on that creates a translated copy of a presentation you choose to translate.

What data we access

When you use Slide Translator, it accesses only the Google Slides presentation you actively choose to translate. It reads the text content of that presentation in order to translate it, and creates a new copy of the presentation containing the translated text.

What we do not do

Slide Translator does not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties. It does not access any other files in your Google Drive beyond the presentation you choose to translate. It does not store your presentation content on any server; translation happens directly through Google's own services at the time you run it.
