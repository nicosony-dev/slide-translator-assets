Privacy Policy for Slide Translator

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Slide Translator is a Google Slides™ add-on that translates the text of a presentation you actively open and choose to translate.

What data we access

When you run Slide Translator, it accesses only the Google Slides™ presentation you currently have open. Specifically, it reads the text content of slides, table cells, and speaker notes in that presentation in order to translate them. Depending on the mode you select, the add-on either replaces the existing text with the translated version, or adds the translated text alongside the original within the same presentation. Slide Translator reminds users to make their own copy of a presentation (via File → Make a copy) before translating, if they wish to preserve the original untranslated version.

How we use this data

The text we access is used solely to perform the translation you request, using Google Translate™ (via Google Apps Script™'s built-in translation service). We do not use your presentation content for any other purpose, including advertising, analytics, or improving unrelated products.

What we do not do

Slide Translator does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. It does not access any other files in your Google Drive™ beyond the presentation you have open and are actively translating. It does not store, log, or retain your presentation content on any external server; translation happens at the time you run it, within your Google account.

Limited Use disclosure

Slide Translator's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

Because Slide Translator does not store your presentation content outside of Google's own systems, there is no separate copy of your data for us to retain or for you to request deletion of. Your presentation remains fully under your control within your Google Drive™.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
