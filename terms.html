What the add-on does

Slide Translator translates the text of the Google Slides™ presentation you currently have open. Depending on the mode you choose, it either replaces the existing slide text with the translated version, or displays the translation alongside the original text within the same presentation. Slide Translator reminds users to make their own copy of a presentation (via File → Make a copy) before translating, if they wish to preserve an untranslated version. Slide Translator does not automatically create or manage copies on your behalf.
